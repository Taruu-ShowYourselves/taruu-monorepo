# Phase 02.1 Plan 01: Free Participate Contract Summary

**Rewrote the participate contract, shared types, api-client call, and participation-history endpoint so no consumer is typed against a payment/GPS/chain-shaped ballot anymore.**

## Accomplishments
- `ParticipateRequestSchema` in `packages/shared/src/contracts/vote.ts` now accepts `{ optionId }` alone — `paymentTxId` and `gpsCoordinates` are gone, along with the now-unused `GpsCoordinatesSchema` import.
- `ParticipateResponseSchema` and `ParticipationSchema` no longer promise `tokensEarned`, `txHash`, `paymentTxId`, `qubikTxHash`, or `gpsCoordinates` — every field left is backed by a `user_votes` column. `ParticipateResponseSchema` gained `alreadyRecorded: boolean` to carry the idempotent-duplicate signal plan 04 will produce.
- `packages/shared/src/types/vote.ts`'s `Participation`/`ParticipationInput` mirror the same shape.
- `votesApi.participate()` in `packages/api-client/src/votes.ts` posts `{ optionId }` only and returns `alreadyRecorded` instead of `tokensEarned`; its test suite was rewritten for the free contract plus a new case asserting the already-recorded success path.
- `/api/user/participations` stopped emitting `paymentTxId`, `qubikTxHash: ''`, and a zeroed `gpsCoordinates` block — confirmed no consumer (`dashboard/page.tsx`) read those fields before removing them.
- `VoteSchema.qubikTxHash` (the vote record, not the ballot) and `CreateVoteRequestSchema.paymentTxId` (vote creation, ₪50, the one working money path) were left untouched, per the plan and the run's critical constraints.
