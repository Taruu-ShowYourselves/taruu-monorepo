/**
 * Anonymous Supabase client (Phase 7, MIG-02).
 *
 * The ANON key with no token. PostgREST evaluates it as the `anon` role, so it
 * reads exactly what a `USING (true)` policy permits and nothing else.

 * eagerly cannot break `next build`.
 */
