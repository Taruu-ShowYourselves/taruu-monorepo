<objective>
Migrate the money-visibility slice — `treasury`, `treasury_transactions`, `issue_coins`,
`issue_coin_holdings` — and prove both halves: the public balances stay public, and a holder's own
position stays theirs.

Purpose: Taruu's pitch is that the money behind executed decisions is visible. That makes this slice
unusual — two of its four tables are *deliberately* world-readable and must stay that way, while the
other two carry per-user positions. Getting the boundary right is the product, not just the security
posture.

SEC-02 already scoped the treasury-transactions endpoint to the caller in `35b0709`. This slice
makes that scoping enforced in the database as well as in the route, so a future refactor of the
route cannot silently reopen the full-ledger enumeration.

Output: two `db/` modules migrated, four call sites migrated, one RLS test over four tables.
</objective>
