Handle the slice that is mostly *not* migrating: NFT minting, notification fan-out, the webhook
replay guard, and merch orders. Migrate the two things that genuinely can — the user's own NFT
gallery and the full push-token CRUD — and give every remaining privileged call site a written
reason.

Purpose: this is MIG-03's slice. The roadmap is explicit that Phase 7 is not "delete
`supabaseAdmin`" — webhooks, cron routes, NFT minting and notification fan-out legitimately require
privileged access. If this slice migrated nothing at all it would still be a success, provided every
call site here can say why it stayed. Most of them will.

It also carries the permanent regression guard for both findings. Plan 07-07 proved them closed
once, by hand. This plan makes them fail the build if they ever reopen.

Output: four `db/` modules handled, six call sites handled, one RLS test over four tables.
</objective>
