<objective>
Close success criterion 3's resident-facing half: surface official responses on the public vote page,
visually unmistakable from Taruu-generated content, with their revision history readable by the
public.

Purpose: "A resident reading a vote can tell Taruu-generated content from an official authority
response at a glance." Everything before this plan makes responses correct and durable; this plan is
the only place a resident ever sees one. Versioning that only the authority can inspect would be
bookkeeping, not accountability — so the history is public here too.

**A. `OfficialResponseBlock.tsx`.** The single resident-facing rendering of an official response.

Header comment: success criterion 3 — a resident must tell this apart from Taruu's own content **at a
glance**, before reading a word of it. Four independent signals carry that, and none of them may be
removed without removing the criterion:
1. An attribution line naming the authority: `תגובה רשמית · {organizationNameHe}`
2. A `מאומת` verified badge next to it
3. An author byline and timestamp: `{authorDisplayName} · {formattedDate}`
4. A visually distinct container — a differently-weighted border and background from every Taruu
   block on the page, built from existing tokens only

Below the body text, a disclosure line stating the platform's relationship to the content, because
proximity implies endorsement otherwise:
`התוכן נכתב ופורסם על ידי הרשות. תַּרְאוּ אינה עורכת, מאשרת או מאמתת את תוכנו.`

- When `revisionCount > 1`, render `עודכן · גרסה {version} מתוך {revisionCount}` with an expandable
  `היסטוריית גרסאות` disclosure listing every prior version — its text, its author, its date and its
  `revisionNote` when present. Use a native `<details>`/`<summary>` so it works without JS and needs
  no new dependency.
- When `publicationState === 'retracted'`, render a clear `התגובה הוסרה על ידי הרשות` marker **above
  the text, with the text still shown**. Comment why the text stays: the public fact is "they said
