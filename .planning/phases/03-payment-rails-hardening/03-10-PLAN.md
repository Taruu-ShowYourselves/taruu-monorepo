<objective>
Produce the two documents COIN-01 needs: the checklist the owner hands an Israeli lawyer, and a file:line inventory of every token claim currently live on taruu.co.il for that lawyer to rule on.

Purpose: COIN-01 blocks COIN-02, COIN-03 and COIN-04, and nothing in this repository can clear it — it is written legal sign-off on the token's status under Israeli securities law, the treasury's custody structure, and what may be claimed to buyers. What this repository *can* do is make the gate cheap and fast to open. A lawyer asked "is our meme coin a security?" in the abstract will bill for a month and answer "it depends". A lawyer handed a concrete list of the sentences that ship today, with a verdict column, can answer in a sitting.

This plan writes no code and needs no credentials. It mirrors `02-02-PLAN.md`'s external-track checklists (`GI-LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md`, `GI-PRIME-CHECKLIST.md`), which are this repository's precedent for handing a question to a human.

Output: `apps/web/docs/COIN-LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md` and `apps/web/docs/COIN-CLAIM-INVENTORY.md`, both flagged PENDING.
